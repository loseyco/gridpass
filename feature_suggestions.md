# New Feature Suggestions

Based on the current GridPass platform ("The Business Operating System for Racing") and the audit of existing features, here are 5 high-impact feature suggestions to drive user engagement and utility.

## 1. Sponsorship Deck Generator
**Goal:** Help racers secure funding by automating the creation of professional sponsorship proposals.
**How it works:**
- Users inputs their stats (pulled from Profile), race schedule, and marketing reach (social media followers).
- Select from premium templates.
- **Output:** A downloadable PDF or a hosted "Live Deck" URL to share with potential sponsors.
**Why:** Solves a major pain point for grassroots racers—selling themselves to sponsors.

## 2. Race Team Budget & Season Planner
**Goal:** Provide financial clarity for race seasons.
**How it works:**
- Users select a series (e.g., ChampCar, Lemons, SCCA) and number of events.
- The tool estimates costs: Entry fees, Tires, Fuel, Travel, Consumables.
- Users can input actuals vs budget.
**Why:** Essential for "Team Hub" functionality. Helps teams split costs accurately (e.g., for "Arrive & Drive" seat pricing).

## 3. QR Asset Management (Shop OS)
**Goal:** Streamline garage inventory and tool tracking.
**How it works:**
- Generate unique QR codes for vehicles, large tools, or spare parts bins.
- Mobile scan to view: Maintenance history, "Checked Out" status, or specs.
- "Where is the 10mm socket?" -> Check usage logs.
**Why:** A "Shop OS" feature that brings enterprise-level tracking to local shops.

## 4. Logistics & Collaborative Itineraries
**Goal:** Coordinate team movement for race weekends.
**How it works:**
- Create a "Race Weekend" event.
- Add team members (Crew, Drivers).
- Assign tasks: "Load Trailer", "Buy Ice", "Tech Inspection".
- Shared timeline/schedule for the weekend (Qualifying times, Drivers meeting).
**Why:** Reduces chaos during events.

## 5. Verified Sim-to-Real Pathway
**Goal:** Bridge the gap between Sim Racing and Real world seats.
**How it works:**
- Integrate with iRacing/ACC stats (manual input or API if possible).
- "Verified Sim Speed" badge on profile.
- Matchmaking prioritizes high-rated sim drivers for real-world endurance teams looking for consistent, safe drivers.
**Why:** Leverages your "Sim Racing" audience and connects them to the "Real World" ecosystem.
