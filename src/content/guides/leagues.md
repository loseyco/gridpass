# Leagues

The **Leagues** app allows you to find and join organized racing series.

## Finding a League

1.  Open the **Leagues** app.
2.  Browse the "Official Leagues" or "Community Leagues".
3.  Click "Join" to see requirements.

## Creating a League

*Currently in invite-only Alpha.*

League owners can:
*   Set schedules and tracks.
*   Manage stewarding and incidents.
*   Automate scoring (integration with iRacing coming soon).

## Stewarding

If you are a steward, use the **Incidents** app to review reported crashes and apply penalties.
