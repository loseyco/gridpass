# Welcome to GridPass

**GridPass** is designed to be the Operating System for your Motorsports Life. It unifies the fragmented world of motorsports—from sim racing leagues to real-world track days—into a single, cohesive platform.

## How it Works

GridPass functions like an OS for your racing career. You have "Apps" for different functions:

*   **Profile**: Your universal racing identity.
*   **Leagues**: Manage or join competitive series.
*   **News**: Aggregated news from across the racing world.
*   **Garage**: (Coming Soon) Manage your virtual and real car collections.

## Getting Started

1.  **Complete your Profile**: Ensure your avatar and bio are up to date.
2.  **Explore Apps**: check out the "News" app to see what's happening.
3.  **Stay Tuned**: We are pushing updates frequently (Alpha/Beta stages).

Need help? Contact us via the **About** app.
