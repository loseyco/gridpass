# Managing Your Profile

Your **GridPass Profile** is your digital racing identity. It's designed to replace your racing CV/Resume.

## Setting Up

1.  **Avatar**: Upload a high-quality headshot or racing helmet photo.
2.  **Cover Photo**: Choose an action shot of your car or a favorite track.
3.  **Bio**: Tell us about your racing history, favorite sims, and real-world experience.

## Public vs. Private

*   **Public Profile**: Accessible via `gridpass.app/u/[username]`. Share this link with team owners, sponsors, or friends.
*   **Private Data**: Your email, phone number, and specific garage details (coming soon) can be kept private.

## Editing

Click the **"My Resume"** app (or "Edit Profile" on your public page) to update your information.
